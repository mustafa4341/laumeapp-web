# Final Image Prompt Set

All reference PNGs were produced with the built-in image generation tool using the following shared direction and screen-specific deltas.

## Shared prompt

```text
Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: full 16:9 desktop website viewport screenshot, no browser chrome
Brand: LAUME light-theme interactive discovery
Style: high-fidelity shippable desktop product UI; tactile, editorial, quiet, premium and restrained
Palette: warm ivory #FBFAF7, white #FFFFFF, paper edge #EDE9E1, navy ink #0F172A, muted slate #64748B, violet #7C3AED only for brand/wax/primary interaction, amber #D7A65A only for discovery
Materials: fine ivory paper grain, subtle embossed contour-map lines, calm pearl-white fog, realistic ivory envelope and deep-violet wax
Typography: elegant editorial serif for discovery/letter copy; clean modern sans-serif for UI
Persistent discovery chrome: LAUME top-left, circular sound control top-right, Keşfi geç bottom-left
Constraints: exact Turkish copy; one dominant focus; no navigation during discovery; no progress bar except the seal hold ring; no marketing cards; no dark theme; no neon; no gaming HUD; no excessive glass; no flowers; no phone frame; no watermark
```

## Screen prompts

```text
01 ARRIVAL — Conceal almost the entire ivory world with soft fog. Show only “Burada bir şey var.” slightly left and below center and one barely visible amber point far right.

02 FIRST TRACE — A 420–500px feathered mouse reveal clears fog at right-center. Show a subtle broken physical trace and “Bir iz.” at left-center. The cursor is visible; there is no flashlight outline.

03 PAPER FRAGMENT — Inside the reveal zone show one small torn paper fragment containing only “…bulacağını…”. Two trace marks continue away. No full letter or envelope.

04 PROXIMITY — Show “18 m” with tiny “yaklaşıyorsun” at left-center. The cursor and subtle marks move toward a warmer upper-right target. Do not imply real GPS.

05 NEAR — Calm fog and reduce visual noise. Show “2 m” and “yakın.” at left-center. Concentrate warm light around a still-concealed target.

06 FOUND — Reveal a small ivory envelope with a realistic deep-violet wax seal at right-center. Show “Buldun.” and “Şimdi aç.” at left-center.

07 HOLD SEAL — Bring the envelope closer. A cursor presses the seal and one thin violet ring is about 65% complete. Show only “Basılı tut.” near the object.

08 PULL LETTER — Open the envelope, split the seal into two restrained pieces and show one letter sheet protruding halfway. Cursor grips the edge. Show “Yukarı çek.” with a subtle vertical cue.

09 LETTER — Center a large deckled ivory letter sheet with readable text: “Bu sadece ilkiydi.” and “Birileri, bir yerde, senin bulman için bir şey bıraktı.” Add a tiny violet wax imprint at the bottom.

10 CONTINUATION — Lower the letter to the right. Show “Yakınında başka ne var?”, text link “Bulmaya devam et →” and note “Uygulamayı açar.” on the left.

11 HOME — Replace discovery chrome with normal navigation. Two-column hero: editorial headline “Bazı şeyler bulunmak için bırakılır.” and concise product description on the left; a real light app-map preview in a slim phone with an ivory envelope and violet seal on the right. Include “Keşfetmeye başla”, “Keşfi yeniden yaşa →” and the restrained steps “Bırak”, “Yaklaş”, “Keşfet”.
```
