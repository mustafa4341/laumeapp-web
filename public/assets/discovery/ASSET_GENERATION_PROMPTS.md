# Final production asset prompt set

Built-in image generation was used. Shared direction:

```text
LAUME light-theme web discovery material asset. Warm ivory handmade stationery, fine natural paper fibers, deep-violet premium wax, straight-on top-down view, restrained neutral lighting, production-quality clean edges, no text, no logo wordmark, no UI, no cursor, no fog, no flowers and no watermark. For isolated layers require genuine transparent alpha with corner alpha 0 and no checkerboard baked into pixels.
```

Asset-specific prompts:

```text
scene-background.png — opaque 16:9 warm ivory paper background with extremely subtle embossed topographic contours; no UI or objects.

physical-trace.png — transparent layer containing only 6–8 interrupted pressed marks curving from lower-left to upper-right.

paper-fragment-blank.png — transparent blank torn ivory paper fragment with deckled edges and restrained contained shadow.

envelope-closed.png — transparent blank closed ivory envelope, back side visible, triangular flap closed; no seal.

envelope-open-rear.png — transparent rear envelope layer containing the back panel and upward-opened flap; no front pocket or letter.

envelope-open-front.png — transparent foreground front pocket with diagonal folds and V-shaped opening; no rear flap or letter.

letter-sheet-blank.png — transparent blank portrait letter sheet with natural deckled edges; no lines or writing.

seal-intact.png — transparent deep-violet organic wax seal with a centered embossed envelope glyph.

seal-crack-01.png — same registered seal with one thin hairline crack reaching toward the center.

seal-crack-02.png — same registered complete seal with a top-to-bottom central fracture passing through the preserved envelope glyph; pieces not yet separated.

seal-piece-left.png — only the registered left half of the broken seal, with jagged fracture and partial glyph.

seal-piece-right.png — only the registered right half of the broken seal, with jagged fracture and partial glyph.
```
